Privacy Policy

Talos Ops — Effective July 28, 2026

This policy describes how Talos Ops (“Talos,” “we”) collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Talos platform, including the Talos Cash Agent (the “Service”).

1. Information We Collect

Account information — your name, business name, and email address when you create an account.

QuickBooks Online data — with your explicit authorization via Intuit’s OAuth consent, we access accounting records needed to operate the Service: invoices, payments, and customer records (including customer names, email addresses, amounts, and dates). We request read scope only for accounting data; we do not access payroll, bank feeds, or banking credentials. You can revoke this access at any time from Settings or from Intuit’s connected-apps page.

Microsoft 365 — with your authorization, a send-mail permission used solely to send messages you have approved, from your mailbox. We do not read, store, or access the contents of your mailbox.

Usage data — standard application logs (actions taken in the Service, timestamps, approximate device/browser information) used for security and reliability.

2. How We Use Information

We use this information solely to provide the Service: mirroring receivables to compute aging and payment behavior, drafting follow-up communications for your review, sending the communications you approve, and producing summaries and metrics for you. We do not sell personal information, share it for advertising, or use your data — or your customers’ data — to train artificial-intelligence models.

3. Service Providers

We use a small set of subprocessors to operate the Service: Vercel (application hosting), Supabase (database hosting, United States), Anthropic (generation of draft text via API; API inputs are not used by Anthropic to train models), Microsoft (sending mail you approve), Intuit (QuickBooks connectivity), and Postmark (transactional notifications). Each processes data only as needed to provide their function.

4. Security

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest. Authorization tokens are stored server-side only and are never exposed to browsers. Each customer’s data is isolated with row-level access controls, and outbound communications require explicit human approval within the customer’s account.

5. Retention and Deletion

We retain data while your account is active. Disconnecting an integration stops further access immediately. On termination or verified request, we delete your data within thirty days, except records we must keep for legal compliance. You may request export of your data at any time.

6. Your Customers’ Information

Contact and balance information about your customers is processed on your behalf and under your instructions to provide the Service to you. If one of your customers contacts us directly about their information, we will refer the request to you and assist as needed.

7. Changes and Contact

We will notify account holders by email of material changes to this policy. Questions or requests: john@talosops.ai.
